Glossary

Key terms

谐音 xiéyīn

Homophony. The sound-alike wordplay behind almost every number code.

弹幕 dànmù

“Bullet comments” that scroll across videos, where 2333 is typed millions of times a day.

网络用语 wǎngluò yòngyǔ

Internet slang. Numbers, letters (yyds, xswl) and memes.

吉利数字 jílì shùzì

Auspicious numbers: 6, 8, 9 and combinations like 168 and 518.

红包 hóngbāo

Red envelope. Amounts like 88, 520 and 1314 carry meaning.

拼音 pīnyīn

The romanization system used to write Mandarin sounds with Latin letters.
