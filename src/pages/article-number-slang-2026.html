Chinese Number Slang 2026: The Codes Everyone Uses

Chinese speakers turn numbers into words through sound-alikes (谐音 xiéyīn). Here is the full list, grouped by mood, with a rating for whether each code is safe to use at work.

How it works: each digit has a Mandarin name (5 = wǔ, 2 = èr, 0 = líng). Say them quickly and they sound like a phrase: 5-2-0 → wǒ ài nǐ, “I love you”.
