About

Why “02333”?

Because the Chinese internet laughs in numbers.

2333 is how hundreds of millions of people type “LOL”. Put a 0 in front, which is often read as 你 (“you”) in number slang, and you get a friendly “you — hahaha!”. That mix of language, humor and numbers is what this site is about.

What we do

  • Decode Chinese number slang with pinyin, examples and usage ratings.
  • Explain the lucky and unlucky number beliefs that shape prices, addresses, phone numbers and launch dates.
  • Connect businesses with vetted specialists for the Chinese-speaking market.
  • Reward the community through monthly contests and prizes.

Editorial standards

Entries are checked against several independent sources and native speakers. Slang changes quickly, so if you spot an error, tell us and we'll fix it. Lucky-number content describes cultural beliefs. It is not a prediction or professional advice.

Independence

02333 is independent. It is not affiliated with any company, stock exchange, platform or media property that uses similar numbers. See our trademark & copyright disclosure.
