Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

What we collect

  • Information you send us: name, email, company and message content when you submit a form (contact, consultation, newsletter, pledge, contest or application).
  • Usage data: if you accept analytics cookies, pages viewed and device or browser type, collected in aggregate.
  • Tool inputs: numbers you type into the Decoder or Lucky Number Analyzer are processed in your browser and not stored by us, unless you choose to email yourself a report. Meme Maker images never leave your device.

How we use it

To answer your request, send the newsletter you subscribed to, run contests, process pledges, match you with service providers (only with your consent) and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

  • Form delivery: submissions are passed to our inbox through a third-party form relay service (FormSubmit).
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense.
  • Analytics (optional): Google Analytics 4.
  • Video: YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, which loads only when you press play).
  • Payments: handled directly by the payment platform you choose (for example PayPal, Stripe, Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee). We never see your card details.

Cookies & advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads to you based on those visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose “Essential only” in our cookie banner, any ads shown are non-personalized and analytics stays off. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or PIPL), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to certain processing. Make a request through our contact form.

Retention & security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them for, then delete them. The site is served over HTTPS.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with an updated date.
