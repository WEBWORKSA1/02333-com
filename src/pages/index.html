FAQ

Quick answers

What does 2333 mean?

It means laughing out loud. It started with the 233rd emoticon on the Chinese forum MOP, a face laughing and pounding the floor. The more 3s, the harder the laugh.

What does 02333 mean?

It's a playful mash-up. In number slang 0 often stands for 你 (“you”), so 0 + 2333 reads as “you — hahaha!”. The same digits are also how some Hong Kong stock codes are written with a leading zero. Read the full story.

Why are 8 and 6 lucky but 4 unlucky?

They're homophones. 8 (bā) rhymes with 发 (fā, prosper), 6 (liù) with 溜/顺 (smooth), and 4 (sì) sounds like 死 (death).

Is the Lucky Number Analyzer scientific?

No. It scores numbers by widely held cultural beliefs. It's fun and useful for understanding how customers may react, but it is not a prediction or advice.
