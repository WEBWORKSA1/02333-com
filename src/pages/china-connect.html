FAQ

Common questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. You get a written plan and a specialist match at no cost. You only pay if you choose to hire a specialist, and you agree the price with them directly.

Do I need to be in China?

No. Most clients are outside China: brands selling cross-border, companies serving Chinese-speaking communities, and importers sourcing products.

How are specialists vetted?

We check licensing where it's required, references and past work. Where a service needs a licence (for example legal or company registration work), it's handled by a firm that holds one.

Are you a law firm?

No. 02333 is an independent referral and information service. We don't give legal, tax or financial advice. Licensed partners handle regulated work.
